# 🏢 Malls Leasing Strategy Dashboard Plan

## 🎯 Objectives Recap

- **Total GLA to lease by end of year** (target).
- **Current pipeline by stages.**
- **Weighted probability of deal closure.**
- **Forecasting where things are heading.**
- **Visual progress toward goal.**
- **Include lease expirations as added targets.**

---

## 📊 Key Charts to Present

### 1. GLA Goal vs Actual vs Forecast (Progress Tracker)

**Chart Type:** Line Chart  
**Description:**

- **X-axis:** Timeline (Weeks or Months up to year-end)  
- **Y-axis:** GLA in sqm  

**Lines:**
- **Target Line:** Linear progression to year-end goal  
- **Actual Closed GLA Line:** Cumulative GLA closed  
- **Forecast Line:** Weighted deal probabilities projecting future GLA (dashed)

**Insight:** Are we on track to hit the target? Will we exceed/fall short?

---

### 2. Pipeline Funnel by Deal Stage

**Chart Type:** Funnel Chart  
**Stages:**

- Initial Contact  
- Proposal Sent  
- Negotiation  
- Legal/Contract  
- Final Approval  
- Closed (won/lost)

**Insight:** Where deals are stuck or dropping off. Focus area for the director’s team.

---

### 3. Weighted Pipeline Projection

**Chart Type:** Bar or Stacked Column  
**Description:**

- Show deals in pipeline with a bar for:  
  - GLA per deal  
  - Weighted GLA (GLA * probability)  
- Aggregate at the top: Expected GLA from pipeline

**Insight:** How much realistically might convert from the pipeline.

---

### 4. Lease Expiry Impact on Target

**Chart Type:** Combo Chart (Bar + Line)  
**Show:**

- Expired GLA (adds to yearly target) – stacked bar  
- Updated Target (line adjusted as new GLA needs emerge)

**Insight:** The dynamic nature of the yearly goal as leases expire.

---

### 5. Monthly Conversion Performance

**Chart Type:** Grouped Bar Chart  
**Groups:**

- Deals started  
- Deals closed  
- Deals dropped  
- Net gain/loss in GLA

**Insight:** How active and effective is the team over time?

---

## 🧭 Filters to Include

- Date Range (default to YTD, allow custom)
- Mall or Region
- Brand Type or Deal Size
- Lease Expiry Window (next 3/6/12 months)
- Team Member or Assignee
